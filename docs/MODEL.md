# Model, pipeline and limits

The model assumes steady, dilute, one-dimensional transport in a fixed rectangular trench, constant diffusion and pseudo-first-order surface reaction. SI units are used internally. The axial concentration satisfies `D d²c/dz² - a c = 0`, with prescribed entrance concentration and a Robin or inert end boundary. Material-specific reactive perimeter divided by area determines `a`. Stable analytic/log solutions are checked against finite differences and independent quadrature in the product tests. Two- and four-face cases and a separately specified end reaction rate are supported.

Planar growth and viscosity supply reaction/diffusion parameters for the theory comparison. The code does not fit k and D to the same SC being evaluated. Composition-dependent properties are preserved. Endpoint concentration ratio and the ratio of spatially averaged top/bottom observation windows are different observables. AR_max is a model/threshold result, not a manufacturing guarantee, void threshold or universal critical aspect ratio.

Quantitative metrology uses `T=(I-DARK)/(REF-DARK)` and `OD=-log10(T)`. REF/DARK acquisition metadata must match. Scalar, finite, positive isotropic pixel calibration and registered observation windows are required. CLAHE is restricted to texture/boundary features. Invalid signal, unsupported calibration, boundary overlap, duplicate image content and stale manifests are rejected or reported explicitly. Calibration evidence is hashed but its scientific truth is not authenticated.

The learning unit is the registered, labeled ROI, not every tile. RF/KRR/SVR model family and parameters are selected inside each outer training fold; X/y scaling stays within the fold. Outer condition LOGO evaluates held-out conditions. Shared batch/specimen/trench/image/measurement/content dependencies crossing conditions are rejected. The final model is separately selected and fitted using all labels; its training predictions cannot be used as independent validation. R², RMSE_nm, MAE_nm, relative bias and sample relative SD (ddof=1) have different meanings.

FE-SEM SC uses paired bot/top observations and supplied SD/correlation. Unavailable ML uncertainty is reported as unavailable, not zero. Monte Carlo propagates supplied means and log covariance, including active/inactive reaction parameters. Condition bootstrap intervals condition on the existing OOF predictions; they do not include full model reselection uncertainty.

The synthetic generator uses chosen property laws, `OD=thickness_nm/450`, camera noise SD 20 counts and synthetic label noise SD 0.4 nm. Its calibration and images share the forward physics. High synthetic scores are expected consistency checks with this construction. They do not establish real optical transfer, camera calibration, generalization or physical validity.

Required experimental work includes original OM/REF/DARK and scale evidence, matched FE-SEM region labels, independent specimen/batch splits, manual comparison measurements, time-resolved planar growth, viscosity and reference D provenance. Growth over the synthetic two-hour image example is extrapolated from a 10–40 minute synthetic planar series; real time stability must be established separately. The model does not establish transient growth, changing geometry, nucleation, depletion, flow, ALD kinetics or equivalence of gas and liquid deposition.

`cvd_cbd demo` and `surrogate.py` retain a separate uncalibrated legacy physics surrogate. Legacy raw/CLAHE features and holdout thickness routines remain for compatibility tests; they are not the calibrated study path. See [input contracts](INPUT_CONTRACTS.md) and [CLI stages](CLI.md).
